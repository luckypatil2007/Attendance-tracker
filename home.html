📐 How the formula works

needed = ⌈(0.75 × total − attended) / 0.25⌉

We find how many classes are still "missing" to hit 75%, then divide by 0.25 because each new class you attend raises your percentage by 1/(total+1) — the ceiling (⌈⌉) rounds up to a whole number.
